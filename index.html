how_it_works · 2026-11-24T05:31:20Z

📖 How MintRadar works

A machine watches every new Solana launch on pump.fun, around the clock. Almost none of them get posted here. This channel only gets the rare launch that clears a published quality filter — and then we score ourselves in public.

The filter (v1.0, frozen in advance). A launch earns an alert one of two ways:
• Proven creator — the creator has at least 3 earlier launches in our records, and their typical 1-hour peak is at least 1.5×.
• Crafted newcomer — a first-time creator with a fully built coin page (art, a real description, links) riding a story that is heating up right now.
Then every candidate must pass the safety screens on its first 20 seconds of trading:
• At least 5 separate buyers — not just the creator.
• Bundled / insider buys under 40% of buy volume.
• No single buyer over 50% of buy volume.
Most hours, zero launches clear all of that. The cap is 15 alerts a day, and the bar never moves to fill the channel.

After an alert: every call is scored by formula at +5 minutes, +1 hour and +24 hours — peak and current price versus the alert moment. Wins, losses and dead coins all post. Scoreboards run twice a day, a recap every morning, and an hourly radar sweep shows what the filter saw — and rejected.

The ledger: every alert is written to a public, hash-chained ledger before it posts here. Entries cannot be edited or deleted without breaking the chain. Check it any time: https://feedpeepxans.github.io/mintradar-observatory/

Use the bot: DM @MintRadarObsBot, tap Check a coin, and paste any contract address. It answers with that coin's card — whether we alerted it, how it scored, and the links. Buttons, no commands to memorize.
